On this New Year, An Appreciation of Twin-Yolked Almonds

How can I express my ardor for them: separable buds of random joy? Intact, exorbitant joy that appears in an anonymous, even militant-looking shell? It is as if they existed solely in a child’s plane where a slug and a butterfly and an anthill are eternally kept. Where there is smallness and slowness and solitude. Sometimes, we find them with an indentation, as if by fingerprint - my species-centric (i.e., literary) interpretation of your mesmeric lease on the mind. I long to anthropomorphize them, to sprinkle upon their existence a little harmless alliteration: Always An Away-Almond Asks After An Asinine Anomaly… Well, maybe not. But the imagination spins. Let it! To never forget the first time each of us saw such a hug of nature. I have taken a pen to one, writing two mysterious English lowercase letters and, just like a child would, encased them in the wax of a tall candle so that they would come up to the surface, again and again, to remind me that the wonder of Spring resides in all (peculiar, silly, anomalous) things.
