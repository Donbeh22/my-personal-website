The Breakthrough of Love 

There is a concluding refrain at the end of Barks' "Essential Rumi" that, though it makes an immediate imprint on the mind  as *important* in youth, somehow seems to unravel itself further and more richly with each passing year: 

I have lived on the lip 
of insanity, wanting to know reasons,
knocking on a door. It opens.
I've been knocking from the inside!

Real value comes with madness
matzub below, scientist above.

Whoever finds love 
beneath hurt and grief 

disappears into emptiness 
with a thousand new disguises.

-Rumi
