Post Title

November 8, 2026

To begin, there is the word. Not as charming as a child's "clog", not as somewhat-understandably euphemistic as "smog", and not as accurate a sensation that invites from the sound of "frog". Was it that I considered this informative if bizarre domain of amateur chefs, mommies-to-be, and cosmetics-junkies had long been milked agape? This pervasive "blog" tab  - and we've already forgotten that it is, like so much that is abbreviated out of memory, a shortened form of Weblog - which implies, doesn't it?, "an accountancy of measurable things". Such a format is dry, or already obsolete, or elemental, or disengaged from a kind of sprightly experiment. I've stayed away for long enough to keep myself convinced of all the aforementioned. The impact, too, that the ill-conceived scroll has on as feeble a brain as ours: information, information, information, give me more information! And Imagination? Neglected, as ever. And yet. Somehow, this 1.0 creature has been compelling enough for so many to generously give it so much of their greatest commodity: their time. Is there some kind of illumination beneath its tail? Or are we, to judge purely by the bubbly skin of the beast, to wade through these things with oars held high, avoiding the leeches down below in the comments section or the even more ancient swamp of the chat room?

Somewhere in between Homer and Twitter, I suppose we have gleaned, the "modern thinking man and woman" among the adventurous blogger. Scoliosis and all. I only hope I can do it justice after having been a naysayer for so long. But first, I come to rename it, redraw its parameters, renew some other vows. I started "Wells" when I was a teenager (what I would like to think is a more attractive if elliptical term); they are snippets, thoughts, manifestos, stories and visions, reading lists, images and composites, music, faces and places. The analog is falling apart, as all good things must: papers have yellowed, some of the ink is fading. They were, and continue to be, my Libra scales. My access to Imagination. My circus freak diary. The basic idea was that we all harbor an endless well of creativity; or at least I must have felt that I did when I began them. So. Welcome visitor, friend, expatriate, alumnus, ghosts to, at last, digitization. I hope this will be some kind of mutual launchpad for whoever stumbles upon it, in whatever dimension,  or a creative haven, or a cerebral-exhibitionist's dream. Not as profound as a book, not as investigative as an essay, and, with whatever it is we keep calling "luck", not as supposedly-irrelevant as a poem. ~HS, 2026
