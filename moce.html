If you could time travel to any time period — what would you choose?

This stays on your screen. Nothing is sent or saved.

Take a look at your own answer

Here’s what to notice in how you explained your choice:

  • Agency vs. observation. Did you talk about taking part in history, like inventing something or joining a movement? Or about watching it safely, as a tourist? That hints at how much risk you’re comfortable with, and how much control you feel you have.
  • Individual vs. collective. Did you focus on personal sensory experiences, like food, music, or fashion? Or on witnessing major turning points in society?
  • Escapism vs. curiosity. Are you running away from something about today, like social media, political tension, or a corporate routine? Or running toward something you find fascinating?
